# Will You Go On A Date With Me? 💌 | Date Proposal Simulator

A romantic, playful, and responsive interactive web app to ask someone special out on a date! Built with pure semantic HTML5, CSS3 animations, and vanilla JavaScript. Ready for instant zero-dependency deployment on **GitHub Pages**, **Vercel**, or **Netlify**.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🐻 **Playful Interactive Mascot**: An animated cute bear with blushing cheeks, breathing animations, and a blooming rose.
- 🏃‍♀️ **Playfully Evasive "No" Button**: Dodges gracefully across the screen when hovered, touched, or clicked, cycling through humorous cute phrases.
- 💖 **Dynamic "Yes" Button**: Grows and glows brighter every time "No" is dodged, complete with heart pulsation and sparkle effects.
- 🎊 **Celebration Extravaganza**: Multi-wave heart-shaped confetti bursts, golden sparkle showers, and custom synthesized chime melodies.
- 🎵 **Ambient Cartoon Melody**: Background music with volume controls and persistent state in localStorage.
- 💌 **Custom Date Idea Planner**: She can choose from quick suggestions (e.g., sunset walk & boba, candlelight dinner & gelato) or write her dream date idea.
- 💬 **Direct WhatsApp Integration**: Automatically formats and opens WhatsApp with the date wish sent directly to your phone number.
- 🏷️ **Name Personalization & URL Sharing**: Customize her name on the fly or share a personalized link via `?name=Sophia`.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive**: Mobile-first glassmorphism design with fluid typography and touch gestures.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start & Deployment

### Run Locally
Open `index.html` directly in any web browser! No installation or build step needed.

### Deploy to GitHub Pages
1. Go to repository **Settings** > **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Source**: `Deploy from a branch`.
3. Choose branch `main` and folder `/ (root)`.
4. Click **Save**. Your site will be live in seconds!
